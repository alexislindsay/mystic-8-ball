Privacy Policy — Mystic 8-Ball

Effective Date: November 2026 • App ID: com.mystic8ball.oracle

1. Zero Personal Data Collection

Mystic 8-Ball — Digital Oracle does not collect, transmit, store, or share any personal information, account credentials, location data, or device identifiers on external servers.

2. Local On-Device Storage Only

Questions typed into the app and the resulting Oracle Chronicle history are stored exclusively on your local device using browser localStorage. You can permanently clear this history at any time using the Clear button inside the app or by uninstalling the application.

3. Device Sensors & Haptics

On supported mobile devices, the application accesses the accelerometer (DeviceMotionEvent) solely in real time to detect a physical shake gesture and triggers standard vibration/haptic feedback. Sensor readings are processed ephemerally in memory and are never recorded or transmitted.

4. No Third-Party Analytics or Advertising

The application contains no advertising SDKs, no third-party tracking pixels, and no in-app purchases.

5. Contact

For questions regarding this Privacy Policy, please visit the project repository at github.com/alexislindsay/mystic-8-ball.
